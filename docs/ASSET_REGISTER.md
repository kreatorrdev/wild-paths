#### Fern Hollow

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: landscape hero photograph for the fictional wilderness portfolio Wild Paths.
Primary request: Create one new photographic scene of Fern Hollow, a sheltered fern-filled clearing in an imagined temperate forest.
Scene/backdrop: A low, intimate hollow enclosed by old conifers and mossy banks. Dense unfurling ferns fill the foreground, with a small natural opening leading gently into the trees. Fine silver mist gives the background depth.
Style/medium: Photorealistic editorial nature photography, natural imperfect textures in fern fronds, damp moss, bark and leaf litter. Restrained cinematic grading, grounded and believable.
Composition/framing: Single wide landscape image, 3:2 aspect ratio. Camera low among the ferns, a quieter and more intimate scene than a tall forest corridor. Keep the fern-filled clearing legible in a centered portrait crop. Allow darker, uncluttered trunks and shadow at the left for possible white heading text added later in the website.
Lighting/mood: Diffused morning light filtered through the canopy, subtle luminous haze, calm and observant.
Color palette: Deep pine green, muted moss, desaturated silver mist and soft warm highlights; detailed shadows.
Constraints: A fictional place, not an identifiable real landmark. One continuous photograph filling the canvas, no collage, no frames, no people, no buildings, no signs, no text, no logo, no watermark. Avoid oversaturation, dramatic sunbeams, fantasy glow, illustrations and CGI.
```

#### Silverthread Falls

```text

Primary request: Create one new photographic scene of Silverthread Falls, a single slender waterfall against dark stone in an imagined temperate forest gorge.
Scene/backdrop: A narrow silver ribbon of water falls down a weathered dark slate cliff into a modest clear pool. Moss clings to damp ledges and fern-covered rocks around the pool. A quiet forest and a trace of spray soften the upper gorge.
Style/medium: Photorealistic editorial nature photography with realistic wet stone strata, moss, fine spray and water movement. Restrained cinematic grading, grounded and believable. The waterfall looks like actual falling water with subtle long-exposure softness, not a luminous painted stripe.
Composition/framing: Single wide landscape photograph in 3:2 aspect ratio. View from low on the opposite rocky bank, waterfall slightly right of center with full fall and pool visible. It should remain identifiable in a centered portrait crop. Leave some quieter shaded rock and foliage on the left for possible website heading copy.
Lighting/mood: Soft overcast daylight enters the ravine from above, delicate silver highlights on the water, deep but detailed shaded stone. Quiet movement and transition.
Color palette: Charcoal slate, muted pine and moss green, silver-white water and cool mist. Compatible with an atmospheric misty forest and lake collection.
Constraints: A fictional place, not an identifiable real landmark. One continuous photograph filling the canvas; no collage, no frame, no people, no bridge, no buildings, no signs, no text, no logos or watermarks. Avoid tropical vegetation, huge epic cascades, turquoise fantasy water, excessive HDR, oversaturation, illustration and CGI.
```

#### Emberstone Summit

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: landscape hero photograph for the fictional wilderness portfolio Wild Paths.
Primary request: Create one new photographic scene of Emberstone Summit, weathered warm rock catching the last evening light above a quiet mountain wilderness.
Scene/backdrop: A broad rugged summit shoulder of fractured iron-stained granite and sparse dry alpine grass, rising to a distinct rocky crest slightly right of center. Beyond it, layers of subdued slate-colored mountains and low valley haze recede to the horizon.
Style/medium: Photorealistic editorial landscape photography with believable geological texture, fine lichen, rough rock grain and atmospheric distance. Restrained cinematic grading, quiet and grounded, consistent with misty temperate forests and lakes.
Composition/framing: Single wide landscape photograph, 3:2 aspect ratio. View from the summit approach, textured foreground stone leads toward the sunlit crest. Keep the main rocks and distant ridges legible in a central portrait crop. Leave a quieter darker mountain slope and hazy horizon on the left where website heading text could later be placed.
Lighting/mood: Last low evening light brushes the rock with muted amber and copper. The shadowed valleys stay cool and hazy; soft clouds in a subdued sky. The restful ending of an imagined journey, no dramatic spectacle.
Color palette: Muted amber stone, warm ochre grass, cool gray-blue distant ridges and subdued pale sky; detailed natural shadows, no vivid orange or pink sunset.
Constraints: A fictional place, not an identifiable real landmark. One continuous photograph filling the canvas, no collage, no frames, no people, no summit cross, no cairns, no buildings, no signs, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Avoid lava, glowing rocks, desert scenery, oversaturation, excessive HDR, illustration and CGI.
```
